# 60-Minute Senior Cardio + Strength
**Low-impact Tabata • Short flat loop bands • Light dumbbells**

A class plan for adults roughly ages 65–80. The format follows the reference structure while using a fresh mix of movement patterns and knee-conscious cardio options.

## Class timeline

| Time | Section | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–10:00 | Warm-up & dynamic mobility | Gradually raise temperature and mobilize ankles, shoulders, hips, and legs |
| 10:00–20:00 | Movement preparation | Rehearse the movements and adjust equipment |
| 20:00–50:00 | Main set | Six 5-minute blocks: 4-minute Tabata + 1-minute strength finisher |
| 50:00–60:00 | Cool-down & stretching | Gradually recover and stretch gently |

### Tabata timer
Each 4-minute block alternates **Exercise A and Exercise B**: 20 seconds work, 10 seconds rest, for 8 rounds total. Each exercise is performed 4 times. Follow with the listed 1-minute strength finisher.

## Equipment and safety
- Stable chairs (preferably without wheels), light dumbbells, short flat closed-loop bands, and an audible interval timer.
- Keep chairs within reach and clear space for small side and diagonal steps.
- Bands are used above the knees or around the wrists only. No long bands, handles, door anchors, or improvised anchors are needed.
- Most participants can aim for about 4–6/10 effort and should still be able to speak a short sentence. The timer sets the rhythm; it does not mean “go all out.”
- Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, chest pressure, or feeling faint. Participants with significant symptoms, recent injury/surgery, or uncertain restrictions should follow their clinician’s guidance.
- Seated options are full participation. Encourage controlled movement, comfortable ranges, steady breathing, and chair support before it is needed.

## 1. Warm-up & dynamic mobility — 10 minutes
1. **Easy march or step-touch** — 2 min. Gradually raise the pulse.
2. **Ankle rocks: toes up / heels up** — 2 min. Mobilize ankles and lower legs.
3. **Shoulder rolls + open/close arms** — 2 min. Upper-body mobility.
4. **Side-to-side weight shift** — 2 min. Prepare for lateral movement.
5. **Hip-hinge rehearsal** — 1 min. Prepare hips and back.
6. **Supported calf raise** — 1 min. Prepare ankles and walking push-off.

Keep this section easy at first and gradually more rhythmic. Standing or seated versions are both appropriate.

## 2. Movement preparation & rehearsal — 10 minutes
Five 2-minute mini-rehearsals. Spend about 45–50 seconds on each movement, leaving time for explanation and equipment adjustments. This is skill practice, not another workout round.

1. **10:00–12:00:** Heel dig + opposite reach; side step-touch + arm sweep.
2. **12:00–14:00:** Alternating hamstring curl; three-direction toe taps.
3. **14:00–16:00:** Seated loop-band hip abduction; loop-band wrist press-apart.
4. **16:00–18:00:** Light-dumbbell hip hinge; seated dumbbell row.
5. **18:00–20:00:** Loop-band side steps; dumbbell hammer curl to chest-level press.

## 3. Main set — six 5-minute blocks
Demonstrate the standard option first, then immediately explain the seated/support option.

### Block 1 — Heel Digs + Hip Control
**Focus:** Knee-friendly cardio and hip strength.

- **A. Forward heel dig + opposite reach** — Cardio/coordination.
  - Standard: Tap one heel forward with toes lifted while reaching the opposite hand forward at chest height; alternate sides. Keep the knee soft and avoid leaning backward.
  - Modification: Sit tall and alternate heel taps with smaller reaches, or tap the toes if lifting the heel is uncomfortable.
  - Progression: Increase rhythm or arm reach slightly while keeping steps quiet and comfortable.
- **B. Seated loop-band hip abduction** — Hip strength; band above knees.
  - Standard: Sit with feet flat and press knees outward a few inches; pause, then return slowly.
  - Modification: Remove the loop or use the lightest band and a smaller range.
  - Progression: Use slightly firmer resistance or hold the open position for 2 seconds; keep the torso upright.
- **1-minute strength finisher: Calf raises.**
  - Standard: Hold a stable chair, rise onto both toes, and lower over about 2 seconds.
  - Modification: Sit and lift both heels with toes on the floor.
  - Progression: Pause 1–2 seconds at the top or lower more slowly.

### Block 2 — Lateral Rhythm + Upper-Body Push/Pull
**Focus:** Low-impact step-touch and shoulder/arm strength.

- **A. Side step-touch + chest-level arm sweep** — Cardio.
  - Standard: Step right and tap the left foot in while opening arms comfortably at chest height; repeat left.
  - Modification: Sit and alternate side toe taps with comfortable arm openings.
  - Progression: Use a slightly wider step or more purposeful arm sweep; keep arms below painful height.
- **B. Loop-band wrist press-apart** — Shoulder stability; loop around wrists.
  - Standard: Keep elbows bent by the ribs and gently press wrists apart, then return with control.
  - Modification: Remove the band or reduce range/tension; practice without resistance.
  - Progression: Pause 1–2 seconds at the widest comfortable point or use slightly more tension.
- **1-minute strength finisher: Dumbbell hammer curls.**
  - Standard: Hold light dumbbells with palms facing inward, curl toward shoulders, and lower slowly without swinging.
  - Modification: Sit, use no weights, or alternate one arm at a time.
  - Progression: Pause near the top or lower over 3 seconds.

### Block 3 — Hamstring Curls + Hip Hinge
**Focus:** Posterior-chain strength and rhythmic leg movement.

- **A. Alternating hamstring curl + arm pumps** — Cardio.
  - Standard: Alternate bending one knee to bring the heel a little backward while gently pumping arms; stay upright.
  - Modification: Sit and slide one heel back under the chair, or alternate toe taps.
  - Progression: Increase rhythm or curl range slightly without arching the back.
- **B. Light-dumbbell hip hinge** — Glutes, hamstrings, and back-line strength.
  - Standard: Hold light dumbbells close to thighs, soften knees, send hips back, and hinge forward a short distance. Stand tall by bringing hips forward and gently squeezing glutes.
  - Modification: Use no weights and hands on thighs, reduce the range, or rehearse a seated hinge.
  - Progression: Lower over a slow 3-count or use slightly heavier weights only if controlled.
- **1-minute strength finisher: Supported hip extension.**
  - Standard: Hold a chair, keep torso tall, and extend one leg a short distance behind without arching the back; alternate.
  - Modification: Stand tall and squeeze both glutes, or sit and perform glute squeezes.
  - Progression: Hold each leg back for 1–2 seconds or increase range slightly without leaning.

### Block 4 — Back Strength + Forward/Back Footwork
**Focus:** Upright rhythm paired with upper-back strength.

- **A. Forward/back toe taps + arm sweep** — Cardio/coordination.
  - Standard: Tap one foot a short distance forward, return, then tap slightly back. Alternate feet and swing arms comfortably; do not pivot on a planted foot.
  - Modification: Sit and alternate forward/back toe taps with small arm sweeps.
  - Progression: Use a slightly quicker rhythm or larger toe taps while staying near support.
- **B. Seated dumbbell row** — Upper-back strength.
  - Standard: Sit near the front of a stable chair and hinge forward slightly with a long back. Pull elbows toward hips, draw shoulder blades back, and lower slowly.
  - Modification: Use no weights, sit more upright, or row one arm at a time with the free hand on the thigh.
  - Progression: Pause 1–2 seconds with elbows back or slow the lowering phase.
- **1-minute strength finisher: Low-diagonal dumbbell raise.**
  - Standard: With very light dumbbells or empty hands, lift arms outward and slightly forward to a comfortable low diagonal; lower with control.
  - Modification: Raise one arm at a time with no weight, or do seated shoulder-blade squeezes.
  - Progression: Gradually increase range toward shoulder height only if pain-free. Do not force arms overhead.

### Block 5 — Clock Taps + Lateral Band Steps
**Focus:** Hip stability and core control, with optional chair support.

- **A. Three-direction clock toe taps** — Cardio/balance practice.
  - Standard: Hold a chair and tap one toe forward, out to the side, and slightly behind; return to center between taps. Alternate feet or complete a short sequence per side.
  - Modification: Sit and tap each toe forward, sideways, then slightly back.
  - Progression: Make taps a little more rhythmic or add a short same-side hand reach. Keep chair support if balance is uncertain.
- **B. Loop-band lateral steps** — Hip strength; band above knees.
  - Standard: Stand near a chair with knees soft and take small side steps without crossing feet, then reverse. Keep gentle band tension.
  - Modification: Use seated band hip abduction or unbanded side toe taps; hold the chair as needed.
  - Progression: Complete 2–3 controlled steps each way or use a slightly firmer band. Avoid deep squats and do not move the band to the ankles.
- **1-minute strength finisher: Seated core brace + alternating heel lift.**
  - Standard: Sit tall with feet flat. Gently tighten abdomen as if preparing to cough, breathe normally, and lift one heel a little; alternate without leaning back.
  - Modification: Practice a gentle abdominal brace while breathing normally, or tap toes.
  - Progression: Add a small opposite-arm reach while keeping the trunk tall and breath flowing.

### Block 6 — Diagonal Step + Full-Body Finish
**Focus:** Simple full-body rhythm, arms, and functional leg strength.

- **A. Diagonal step-and-reach** — Cardio/whole-body coordination.
  - Standard: Step one foot diagonally forward and out, return to center, then switch sides. Reach hands forward at chest height; keep feet low and avoid twisting.
  - Modification: Sit and alternate diagonal toe taps with a forward reach, or use unweighted arm reaches only.
  - Progression: Widen the diagonal step or increase rhythm slightly. Do not add pivots, jumps, or deep knee bends.
- **B. Dumbbell hammer curl + chest-level press** — Upper-body strength.
  - Standard: With light dumbbells and palms inward, curl toward shoulders, then press forward only to comfortable chest height. Bring weights back in and lower with control.
  - Modification: Sit and do curls only, use no weights, or alternate one arm at a time. Omit the press if shoulders are uncomfortable.
  - Progression: Slow the return or increase range slightly; keep weights light and do not lean back.
- **1-minute strength finisher: Sit-to-stand from a firm chair.**
  - Standard: Feet about hip-width apart. Lean forward from hips and stand, then sit slowly with control. Use chair arms as needed.
  - Modification: Use chair arms, practice a partial stand, or do a forward weight shift and seated glute squeeze.
  - Progression: Use less hand assistance only if steady and pain-free. Avoid adding dumbbells if knees are sensitive or movement is not secure.

## 4. Cool-down & stretching — 10 minutes
Each stretch should feel mild, not sharp or forceful. Do not bounce or push into joint pain. Keep a chair available for standing stretches.

1. **Slow heel taps + relaxed breathing** — 2 min. Gradually reduce movement.
2. **Supported calf stretch** — 2 min, about 1 min per side. Face a wall or sturdy chair, step one foot back, keep heels comfortably grounded, and lean forward slightly. Seated option: ankle pumps or a gentle toes-up position.
3. **Seated hamstring stretch** — 2 min, about 1 min per side. Sit near the front of a chair, extend one leg with heel down and toes up, and hinge forward slightly with a long back. Keep the knee bent more if needed.
4. **Chest opener + shoulder cross-body stretch** — 2 min, about 1 min each. Open arms gently, then bring one arm across the chest and hold lightly. Keep the range comfortable.
5. **Upper-back reach + quiet finish** — 2 min. Reach arms forward at chest height to gently spread shoulder blades, relax, and breathe easily. Seated is fine.

## Instructor notes: safety, intensity, and flow
- **Knee-friendly coaching:** Favor quiet foot taps, small lateral steps, upright posture, and shallow knee bend. Avoid jumping, fast pivots, deep squats, and repeated high knee lifts.
- **Effort:** Most participants can aim for 4–6/10 effort and still say a short sentence. Participants who need more support can work at a lower intensity.
- **Equipment:** Choose dumbbells light enough for controlled movement. Place loops above knees for hip work and around wrists for press-aparts only. Inspect bands for tears; remove a band if it rolls or feels uncomfortable.
- **Breathing:** Breathe continuously; generally exhale through effort and inhale as you return. Do not hold the breath during strength work.
- **Participation:** Seated variations are full participation. Participants may pause, stay seated, use both hands on a chair, or skip any move that causes pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms.
- **Knee-pain alternative:** Use seated heel taps, seated hip abduction, unweighted arm sweeps, and seated core-brace work in a pain-free range rather than pushing through pain.
